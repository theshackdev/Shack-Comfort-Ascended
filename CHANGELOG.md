# Changelog

## 1.0.1
- The hammer's build menu shows which pieces give comfort: a gold **+N** on the piece's icon, and the piece info says how much and which kind it counts as (only the best of each kind counts). Each player can turn it off ("Show comfort in build menu").
- Works together with 1.0.0: players and the server can be on either.

## 1.0.0
- First release: comfort objects under a roof link into chains within 10 m of each other, and a chain counts as one. Moving dots show the links while you look at or place a comfort object.
